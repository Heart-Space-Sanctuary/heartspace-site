Founder & Guide | Visionary Mystic & Intuitive Channel

Aluminea

Aloha! I'm Aluminea and I would love to share a piece of how Heart Space Sanctuary came to be. I received the vision of Heart Space in the Fall of 2022 at a time when I felt defeated and alone in my single-parenting and professional journeys. Before 2020, my private practice, Blossoming Lotus, was thriving with 2 locations, one in Seal Beach, CA and one on Kauai. In March 2020 when the pandemic occurred, I lost both streams of income and my partner had resorted to old patterns of addiction and abuse. I decided to go into hiding physically and energetically to protect my son in utero and I. With no financial or family support, I was challenged to lean into my spiritual practice of trust and surrender more than ever before. The two most common affirmations I accepted as true were, "In every moment we have everything we need," and, "Everywhere my son and I go we are surrounded by unconditional love." I coded these affirmations into my mind, body, and soul and they proved to be true in my external reality. Neighbors left brand new baby items on my porch, friends helped get car seats and strollers, and we connected with soul family in love, bliss, and joy everywhere we went. We continued to live with these knowings in our hearts as we lived off-grid and in harmony with the land in Hawaii.

It was on the Big Island that the vision of heart-centered community came through during a virtual energetic alignment session I was sharing with someone at the time. I had been asking my guides and my Higher Self to show me how I could be of highest service to the collective in my daily meditations. In this vision, I saw multiple family-friendly wellness centers in cities, suburbs, and county land all over the world. I felt waves of chills coursing through my entire body while simultaneously experiencing a deep knowing that committing my life completely to one of service was the path I was being guided on.

From there, I reached out to my dear soul brother Keegan to ask if he would join me on the journey of visioning these sanctuaries into existence, as I knew he had also taken a similar vow of selfless service to the collective. He said, "Yes!" without hesitation, and so Heart Space was born.

It has been and continues to be my sacred honor to be a space holder, intuitive guide, and visionary channel for our community. My sole intentions for my one-on-one sessions and community gatherings is to create experiences for you to express and embody unconditional love for yourself, your family and friends, and our greater collaborative community. If you feel called into a similar vision of community, I look forward to when our paths cross and we can commune with unconditional love in our hearts to see what is possible for creating a loving and peaceful world for generations to come.

Aluminea guides group and individual offerings on the Central Coast of California and virtually

Founder & Guide | Sacred Space Holder & Ceremonialist

Keegan Brady

With over a decade of experience in wellness facilitation and eight years as a counselor, Keegan has dedicated his career to supporting individuals on their paths to healing, growth, and transformation. His approach is rooted in compassion, depth, and a profound respect for the mind-body-spirit connection.

Keegan specializes in creating safe, nurturing spaces that allow both individuals and groups to explore emotional and physical well-being. As an Integrative Health Practitioner, he draws from a wide range of holistic modalities tailored to each person's unique needs, always prioritizing balance, harmony, and long-term wellness.

A certified Kambo Facilitator, Keegan guides participants through the Amazonian Kambo ritual, ensuring a secure and supportive setting for this powerful cleansing and healing experience. His work as a Ceremonial Musician enhances these spaces further, using music as a transformative tool to facilitate emotional expression, connection, and deep inner work.

Keegan is also a Trauma-Informed Breathwork Facilitator, leading individuals through intentional breathing techniques designed to release trauma and foster resilience. His credentials as a QiGong and Reiki Master Teacher allow him to offer energy-based healing sessions and instruction, empowering others to access and cultivate their own healing potential.

With a background in drug and alcohol dependency and mental health counseling, Keegan brings a compassionate, grounded presence to his work in detox and inpatient treatment centers. His eight years of experience in this field have shaped a trauma-aware and client-centered approach that supports lasting recovery and emotional wellness.

In addition to his healing and counseling work, Keegan is also a skilled artisan, crafting consumable and holistic wellness products such as aura sprays and anointing oils. These creations, informed by spiritual sciences, are designed to support wellness on both the physiological and energetic levels.

Committed to serving his community, Keegan's work as a wellness retreat facilitator and ceremonialist is driven by a dedication to helping others achieve holistic health and personal growth. His diverse skills and compassionate approach support individuals on their journeys to healing and self-discovery.
