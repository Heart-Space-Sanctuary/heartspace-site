Village Haven Mission

Village Haven empowers single moms with meaningful job opportunities while helping households find trusted support for everyday life. By creating connections built on trust, we are reviving the village where families support one another, and everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

Give to the Village Haven Mission Visit Village Haven

Across the country, millions of single mothers carry the full responsibility of providing for their families while also being the primary caregivers for their children. The emotional weight is immense, and the financial pressures are often overwhelming. For many, the hardest part is feeling like they have to do it all alone.

Village Haven was created to help revive something humanity has always needed: the village.

Village Haven is a community-powered platform where families help families. It connects single moms with flexible, meaningful work opportunities while helping families find trusted support within their community. Moms may also bring their children with them while completing certain jobs, allowing them to earn income without sacrificing precious time with their kids. Our goal is to create a system where parents can earn income, care for their children, and build genuine relationships with the families they serve.

Safety and trust are foundational to Village Haven. Every provider who joins the platform must complete a background check. To ensure that financial barriers never prevent a deserving mom from joining, we are launching the Background Check Scholarship Fund so that donors can help sponsor these checks and open the door for moms to participate.

This campaign is raising $500,000 in seed funding to launch and grow Village Haven. These funds will help us:

  • Sponsor background checks for single moms joining the platform
  • Expand awareness so families can discover Village Haven
  • Build and strengthen the Village Haven platform and technology
  • Grow the team and community that helps weave local villages together

Village Haven is more than a platform. It is a movement to restore the idea that no parent should have to raise a family alone.

When you support Village Haven, you are helping create opportunities for single moms, trusted connections for families, and stronger communities for everyone.

Together, we can remind every family that they have a village.

Help Us Build Our First Sanctuary For Single Mothers

Heart Space Sanctuary was created from a simple belief: mothers and families deserve to be supported by a loving community.

The long-term vision is for Village Haven LLC to generate sustainable revenue that helps fund the work of Heart Space Sanctuary.

As Village Haven grows, profits are directed back into Heart Space Sanctuary to help create our first physical sanctuary dedicated to supporting mothers and newborns.

Our dream is to build a place where single mothers can bring their babies into the world safely and be held in community during the sacred early months of life.

The Heart Space Sanctuary postpartum village will offer:

  • A safe and peaceful place for mothers to give birth naturally with the support of midwives and doulas
  • A supportive community to help care for mother and baby
  • Nourishing meals, rest, and emotional support
  • Guidance from experienced caregivers and community elders
  • Three months of postpartum care so mothers can recover, bond, and begin their journey with stability and love

The first months after birth are one of the most vulnerable and important times in a family’s life. No mother should have to navigate that season alone.

Heart Space Sanctuary exists to help rebuild the village that once surrounded mothers and children.
