Heart Space Sanctuary is devoted to building real connection.

Here, people come together to learn, share, and support one another through practices that foster compassion, clarity, and mutual respect.

In a world where differences in belief, culture, and identity often create separation, we honor each person’s unique life experiences.

When we meet our differences with curiosity and acceptance, something shifts. People feel safe and encouraged to share what makes them come alive.

We would love to get to know you! You are invited to join us:

  • In our virtual Gatherings
  • In our mission to revitalize sustainable community living
  • In supporting the building of physical sanctuary spaces
